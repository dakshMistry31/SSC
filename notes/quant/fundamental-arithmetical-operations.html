🛠️ 4. Order of Operations

For a mixed expression, follow the mathematical order of operations. Brackets are handled first; then powers/roots where present; then multiplication/division from left to right; finally addition/subtraction from left to right.

Brackets → Powers/Roots → Multiplication/Division → Addition/Subtraction
Example: 8 + 12 ÷ 3 × 2
12 ÷ 3 = 4, then 4 × 2 = 8, then 8 + 8 = 16.

➗ 5. Fractions

Addition/Subtraction

Convert to a common denominator, then add or subtract numerators.

a/b + c/d = (ad + bc)/bd
a/b − c/d = (ad − bc)/bd

Multiplication

a/b × c/d = ac/bd

Division

a/b ÷ c/d = a/b × d/c
Example: 2/3 ÷ 4/5 = 2/3 × 5/4 = 10/12 = 5/6.

🔢 6. Decimal Operations

  • For addition/subtraction, align decimal points.
  • For multiplication, multiply as whole numbers first and then place the decimal according to the total number of decimal places.
  • For division, make the divisor a whole number by shifting the decimal in both numbers by the same number of places.
Example: 4.25 + 0.75 = 5.00.

✏️ 7. Solved Examples

Example 1: Evaluate 18 − 6 × 2.
Multiplication first: 6 × 2 = 12. Then 18 − 12 = 6.
Example 2: Evaluate (24 + 6) ÷ 5.
Bracket first: 30 ÷ 5 = 6.
Example 3: −8 + 13 = 5. Different signs → subtract magnitudes: 13 − 8 = 5, and keep the sign of 13.

⚠️ 8. Common Mistakes

  • Performing addition before multiplication in a mixed expression.
  • Changing the sign incorrectly when multiplying or dividing negative numbers.
  • Adding fractions by simply adding denominators.
  • Misplacing decimal points.
  • Forgetting to simplify the final fraction.

🔄 10. Quick Revision

  • Follow the correct order of operations.
  • Same signs in multiplication/division give a positive result.
  • Different signs give a negative result.
  • For fraction division, multiply by the reciprocal.
  • Align decimal points for addition/subtraction.
