📖 1. What is Number System?

Number System is the study of different kinds of numbers and the relationships between them. For CHSL, you should be comfortable with whole numbers, integers, fractions, decimals, factors, multiples, divisibility, remainders and basic number properties.

🧮 4. Important Properties

Even and Odd

Even numbers are divisible by 2. Odd numbers are not divisible by 2.

Even ± Even = Even   |   Odd ± Odd = Even   |   Even ± Odd = Odd
Even × any integer = Even   |   Odd × Odd = Odd

Prime Numbers

A prime number has exactly two positive factors: 1 and itself. Examples: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11.

Remember: 2 is the only even prime number. 1 is neither prime nor composite.

🛠️ 5. Divisibility Rules

  • 2: Last digit is 0, 2, 4, 6 or 8.
  • 3: Sum of digits is divisible by 3.
  • 4: Last two digits are divisible by 4.
  • 5: Last digit is 0 or 5.
  • 6: Number is divisible by both 2 and 3.
  • 8: Last three digits are divisible by 8.
  • 9: Sum of digits is divisible by 9.
  • 10: Last digit is 0.
  • 11: Difference between sums of alternate digits is 0 or a multiple of 11.

✏️ 6. Solved Examples

Example 1: Is 4,572 divisible by 3?

Digit sum = 4+5+7+2 = 18. Since 18 is divisible by 3, 4,572 is divisible by 3.
Example 2: What is the remainder when 157 is divided by 9?

157 = 9 × 17 + 4. Therefore, remainder = 4.
Example 3: Which is the smallest prime number?

2. It has exactly two factors, 1 and 2.

⚠️ 7. Common Mistakes

  • Calling 1 a prime number.
  • Forgetting that 0 is a whole number but not a natural number under the usual CHSL convention.
  • Using the divisibility-by-3 rule for 9 without checking the correct divisor.
  • Confusing factors with multiples.
  • Ignoring negative integers when a question asks about integers.
